05Process

How it
works

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Send the date, the venue and what you need. I come back with availability and a quote.

  2. 02

    Brief

    We lock the scope — set times, access, how many frames, and where the images will run.

  3. 03

    The shoot

    I work light and stay out of the way. Bay Area as standard, available to travel.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    A selected, edited gallery. Additional frames and rush turnaround on request.
